In this video, we'll walk through how to create and manage subscriptions billing in Field. You'll learn what subscription billing is, how to create subscription packages, how to assign subscriptions to customers, and how to view and manage active subscriptions. So, what is subscription billing? Subscription billing allows you to create recurring billing packages based on your services and booking frequency. These packages can be sold directly to customers or offered online during booking. With Field's subscription billing, you can offer discounted subscription services and automatically schedule recurring jobs, helping you build predictable revenue and keep customers coming back. Let's start by creating a new subscription package. From the main menu, navigate to new, then select subscription package. Step one, create the package. First, enter a name and description for your package. Next, set your pricing. Choose how often the package charges, such as weekly, monthly, or yearly, and enter the price for that billing cycle. You can also add commitment options. Commitment options allow you to offer customers a discount when they commit to a longer subscription period upfront. Instead of paying month-to-month, customers can choose to pay for several months in advance, which often comes with a discount. If you want customers to purchase this package online, turn on the option to make it available for self- purchase. You can also enable it as an upsell during booking. If these options are turned off, the package can only be sold from within the CRM. Next, choose whether this package includes automatic scheduling. If scheduling is enabled, select the booking frequency for this package. If you do not want automatic scheduling, you can leave this turned off and move to the next step. Now, select which services are included in this package. You can also apply discounts or included price reductions. For example, a customer who subscribes to a monthly $99 package might receive 20% off all services and one free add-on each month. Once everything is set up, review your package details and click save. Let's look at how to assign a subscription to a customer from within the CRM. From the main menu, select new, then choose subscription. Choose an existing customer or click the plus icon to add a new one. Select an existing subscription package or create a new one if needed. Next, choose when billing should begin and how often it should occur. And lastly, review the subscription details and confirm the setup. Process the payment and activate the subscription. Once activated, you'll be able to view the customer's subscription details. Viewing active subscriptions. Open the transactions tab. Select subscriptions, then view the list of active subscriptions. Click on any customer to see their subscription details. Viewing subscription packages. Click the drop down and choose packages. Here you'll see all available subscription packages.
