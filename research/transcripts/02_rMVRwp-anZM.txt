In this video, we'll walk through how route optimization works in Field. You'll learn how routes are optimized, what customers see when booking, how administrators can track progress, and how workers use routes inside the worker app. How route optimization works. Route optimization helps you organize your team's day more efficiently by automatically mapping out the best route for scheduled jobs. When route services are added to the calendar, they sit inside a designated route for that day. At the start of the route, the worker simply swipes start route. Once started, Field automatically optimizes the route, arranging jobs and stops in the most efficient order. The entire day is then mapped out, showing each job and stop in sequence. Workers proceed through each job using the normal job workflow until the route is complete. To begin using route optimization, you'll need to make sure it's activated in your toolkit. Then create a new route service or confirm you already have one set up. Here's how to set up a route service. From the dashboard, go to settings, then select services. Next, click the new button at the top right and choose service. Give your service a name that clearly describes it. Select route. Be sure to select a category this service is linked to. Once selected, click save, then click publish. You've now successfully created a route service within your account. Let's run through an example of how this works in real time from the customer's perspective. When customers book a route service online, they select the day of the week they want service. They do not select a specific time. For this example, four customers are booking a route service for March 5th. Instead of choosing a time slot, they may see something like anytime Thursday. If you'd like to customize this wording, you can navigate to settings, customer hub, style, text, then select routes text. Once you've made your changes, click save. Now, let's look at how routes function inside the worker app. On the day of work, the worker opens the day's route and taps start route. As soon as the route begins, route optimization automatically rearranges the jobs into the most efficient order, helping reduce travel time and save fuel. The app then guides the worker to the first stop on the optimized route. From there, each job follows the normal workflow. The worker can take before and after photos, complete checklists, message the client, and finish all required steps just as they would with any other job. Once the final job is completed, the route closes automatically, marking the entire day's route as finished. How administrators track route progress. As an administrator, you can monitor how far along a worker is on their route at any time. To do this, open the route from the calendar. Inside the route overview, you'll see completed jobs and remaining jobs clearly listed. You can click on any job to view its details. You'll also see a completion percentage at a glance, allowing you to quickly understand overall route progress. Now that you understand how route optimization works, we recommend creating a test route and walking through the full workflow with your team. This ensures everyone understands how to start a route, complete jobs, and track progress efficiently.
